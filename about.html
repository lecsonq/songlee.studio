Song Lee is a visual artist currently based in Seoul. Her work explores the intersection of photography, printmaking, and painting, often blurring the boundaries between two-dimensional and three-dimensional forms. Through her practice, she investigates the materiality of image and its relationship to space.
🞻
This website presents a range of visual outcomes arising from my ongoing interest in the image. Its structure—PAGE, OBJECT and SPACE—is organised around questions of scale and the distinct processes through which images are made.
PAGE brings together works conceived as individual sheets, in which the language of photography or painting functions as both medium and subject. These inherently flat surfaces sometimes extend into three-dimensional forms through the use of supporting structures.
In OBJECT, the process of image-making moves in the opposite direction: three-dimensional objects become two-dimensional images through photography. The objects themselves, together with their interplay with light, form the basis of the photographic image, as tangible forms are translated onto seemingly illusory surfaces.
SPACE presents works in their installed contexts. Here, the works respond to the physical conditions of their surroundings and often involve interaction with the audience.
